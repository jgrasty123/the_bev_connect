How to sell beer online

Why breweries can't just add a checkout button.

Selling beer online isn't a website problem — it's a licensing problem. Here's what actually stands between your taproom and a customer's doorstep.

The retail license

Direct-to-consumer beer sales require a retail license most breweries don't hold — and getting licensed as an online alcohol retailer is a business of its own.

State-by-state compliance

Every destination state has its own rules on shipping alcohol, plus age verification and reporting requirements on every single order.

Alcohol fulfillment

Beer is heavy, fragile, and carrier-restricted. Shipping it takes an alcohol-approved carrier program and a warehouse built for it — a true alcohol 3PL.

The shortcut: partner with a channel that already holds the license, runs the compliance, and ships beer every day. That's exactly what we built.

FAQ

Selling beer online, answered.

How can a brewery sell beer online?

Most breweries can't ship DTC on their own license, so the practical path is partnering with a licensed retail channel. We build a branded storefront for your beer, our licensed retail partner purchases at wholesale and acts as seller of record on every order, and our Ventura, CA fulfillment center handles age verification, compliance reporting, pick & pack, and shipping.

What does it cost?

Nothing. No build fee, no monthly fee, no fulfillment bills. We earn our margin on the retail sale; you collect wholesale revenue on every case that moves, with a monthly sales report showing what sold and where it shipped.

Do I need an alcohol shipping license?

Not in this program. The licensed retailer is the seller of record on every order — no license work, website build, checkout, or shipping compliance on your side. Your only task is pointing a "Shop Online" button at the storefront.

Where can my beer ship?

Orders ship compliantly to most U.S. states from Ventura, CA on discounted UPS and GLS rates, with same-day shipping on weekday orders. Eligible destinations are confirmed at checkout on every order.

Is this exclusive? Am I locked in?

No and no. The storefront is ours to run — you stay free to sell and link anywhere else you like. Programs are month-to-month.

What beer sells best online?

Regional flagships fans can't get locally, beer-of-the-month clubs and subscriptions, limited and barrel-aged releases, mixed 12 & 24-packs built for shipping, and gift sets kitted in-house with engraving available.
